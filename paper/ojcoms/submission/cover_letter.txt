We submit the manuscript "Quota-Constrained Multi-Rate Synchronization in Federated Learning: Gain Bound, Peak-Load Control, and Schedule Leakage" for consideration as an Original Manuscript in the IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society. The article is 18 pages in the IEEE Open Journals template and is accompanied by a supplement, a graphical abstract, and the figures as separate files.

Summary. The article studies a scheduler for cross-silo federated learning over constrained links that synchronizes groups of layers at different rates under a byte quota. It gives a closed-form bound on how far any per-tier schedule can lower a measured surrogate of client drift below that of a uniform period, shows that staggering the tiers controls the payload of a single round, and analyzes the schedule as a privacy release and as a timing side channel under client-level differential privacy. The central result is negative and is stated as such: in seven settings the scheduler shows no relevant accuracy gain over the best uniform period at matched bytes. In the six settings where the bound is small, it predicts this outcome; in the seventh, the bound is large and there is still no gain. We believe the article fits the journal's topics "Big Data and Machine Learning for Communications" and "Cloud Computing, Edge Computing, and Internet of Things".

Main differences from that version.
1. Writing. The abstract and the Introduction were rewritten, a Related Work section with a positioning table was added, and the whole article and supplement were edited for grammar, terminology, and IEEE style. The title was shortened.
2. References. Every entry was checked against its source and completed (place and month for proceedings; volume, issue, pages, and month for journals; DOIs; arXiv numbers). Five missing citations were added to the article, which now has 57 references, and eight were added to the supplement, which now has 18.
3. Corrections. While revising, we checked every statement against the released logs and code and corrected several sentences, table notes, and captions. No result changed. The bootstrap confidence intervals were regenerated with the seeds read in ascending order, so that they do not depend on the order of the log files; this changes the limits of the intervals in Tables 3 and 4 and in Table S-II, mostly in the last digit, and one permutation p-value in Table 9 (0.009 to 0.010). The settings of two baselines are now stated, and three supplement captions were corrected.
4. Format. The manuscript now follows the IEEE Open Journals template and includes the required graphical abstract.
